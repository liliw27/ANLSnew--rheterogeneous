Multi-compartment                                         heuristic is evaluated on data from a Chinese petroleum transportation company and compared against
Multi-trip                                                exact results from a MILP model and lower bounds from a column generation approach. In addition, we
Split-delivery                                            perform sensitivity analysis on different problem features, including the number of vehicles, products,
